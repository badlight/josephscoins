About

A living catalogue of an ancient coin collection.

This site presents a personal collection of Roman Imperial, Roman Provincial, and Byzantine coinage. Catalogue details are drawn directly from the collection's JSON record, so the gallery updates as the collection grows.

Dates and attributions reflect the collector's catalogue records and references.
